# About the IWC Lab

The IWC Lab is a sandbox next to the [Intergalactic Workflow Commission](https://iwc.galaxyproject.org). Workflows here are experimental: they are tested and deployed the same way IWC's are, but they have **not** been through IWC review.

## What's here

- Workflows written in Galaxy's Format-2
- Workflows being tried out before they are proposed to IWC

## Where they come from

Everything on this site is built from [galaxyproject/iwc-lab](https://github.com/galaxyproject/iwc-lab). Workflows merged there are published to the [iwc-lab-workflows](https://github.com/iwc-lab-workflows) GitHub organization and registered on [Dockstore](https://dockstore.org/organizations/iwc-lab-workflows), which is where "Run in Galaxy" loads them from. Nothing here is installed on the usegalaxy.\* servers.

For reviewed, production-ready workflows, use the [IWC workflow library](https://iwc.galaxyproject.org).
